Frequently Asked Questions

அடிக்கடி கேட்கப்படும் கேள்விகள் Common Questions

Answers about our two-wheeler tyre sales, on-order sourcing for other vehicles, and vulcanizing services.

We maintain a regular inventory of popular motorcycle and scooter tyres in standard sizes and reputed brands. You can visit our shop in Achampathu or call us directly to check current size availability.
If you need a specific car, JCB, truck, lorry, or custom tyre size that is not immediately in shop, contact us with your vehicle type, tyre size, and required quantity. We check our trusted distribution sources and arrange a suitable tyre according to your order.
We inspect the tyre first to evaluate the severity and location of the damage. If the structural cords and casing are eligible, we provide high-durability thermal vulcanizing or patchwork repair, helping you save money over replacing the tyre.
